Notlar ve uyarılar (araştırmacının): 1) Solonenko & Mobley 2015 is paywalled. Its a and b tables, Chl and model parameters come from two secondary transcriptions: the Dstl (Williamson & Hollins) figshare workbook and the 2026 PyPI package 'jerlov'. They agree exactly for types I-III. S&M's Kd/KdH and the Kd(a,b) constants rest on a single secondary source or on sources that conflict. I did not find which phase function or backscatter fraction S&M used. By my check, S&M's Table-3 Bs/Bl are inconsistent with the b column for types I and IA. 2) The Austin & Petzold 1986 values come from Mobley's authorised reprint (Light and Water Table 3.15), not from the paywalled Opt. Eng. paper. The Jerlov 1976 values in the notes are secondary. 3) The seawater b rows are my own computation with a Python port of the official ZHH2009 MATLAB code (T=20 C, S=38.4); the paper has no table. My port did not exactly reproduce the paper's quoted temperature sensitivity (4.2%/7.0% vs 3.7%/5.5%). Morel 1974 values are 10-14% higher than ZHH. 4) ASTM G173 is a reference spectrum: AM1.5 (zenith 48.19 deg), US-average atmosphere, on a 37 deg tilted plane. It is NOT a noon Mediterranean sky, where the lower zenith gives more and bluer direct light. Treat it as a reference shape; matching site and season would need a SMARTS run. 5) Pope & Fry is pure fresh water. S&M absorption uses a different pure-water absorption in the red (below P&F at 650-700 nm), so do not mix a_w sources within one IOP set. 6) Petzold values below a few degrees and ab
